## 1. Android developer verification goes global in January 2027 — and you can now register apps from CI

- **Working title:** Android Developer Verification Goes Global in January 2027: Register Your Apps From CI Before the Rollout
- **Why now:** Google announced on **19 Nov 2026** that the global rollout of Android developer
  verification **begins January 2027** across seven distributors (Google Play, Galaxy Store, Honor,
  Oppo, Xiaomi, Transsion, Vivo), expanding to **all installation sources** on certified devices
  over several months. Same post ships the **Android Developer ID Status API** and **Android
  Developer Console API** for CI pipelines, and raises limited-distribution accounts from
  **20 to 50 authorized devices**.
  Source: https://developer.android.com/blog/posts/android-developer-verification-2027
- **Reader value:** The practical registration + automation playbook for indie and small-team
  Android devs: how to check package registration status, which account type you need if you ship
  outside Play, how to wire the two new APIs into GitHub Actions so unregistered builds fail the
  pipeline, and what the "advanced flow" means for your beta testers.
- **Target phrase:** "android developer verification 2027"
- **Links to:** `android-developer-verification-sept-30`, `google-play-12-tester-rule-indie`,
  `developer-id-certificate-expiry-2027-mac-apps`
- **Overlap flag:** the Sept 9 post covered the **Sept 30 / four-country** launch. This is a
  different event (global rollout date + the new automation APIs). Angling it at **CI automation**
  keeps it from re-treading the old post, and it gives that post a strong inbound link.

## 2. Google Play's April 2027 foldable rules: Tier 3 adaptive quality and 3 foldable screenshots

- **Working title:** Google Play's April 2027 Foldable Requirements: The Tier 3 and Screenshot Checklist
- **Why now:** Announced **18 Nov 2026**. From **April 2027**, apps must meet **Tier 3 "Adaptive
  ready"** quality guidelines for foldables or they **cannot publish or update on Google Play** and
  may lose store visibility, and must ship **3 foldable screenshots** alongside the standard
  **2 phone screenshots**. Foldable screenshot upload is **live today** in Play Console. A second
  milestone lands **August 2027**: target **API level 37 (Android 17)**, with orientation and
  resizability opt-outs removed. Google cites **580M+ large-screen devices**.
  Source: https://developer.android.com/blog/posts/google-play-adaptive-quality-standards
- **Reader value:** A do-it-this-week checklist: the exact aspect ratios required (1:1 and 4:3 inner
  displays, 10:16 folded outer, optional 16:9/16:10 landscape), how to capture compliant shots from
  the Android Emulator instead of buying a Z Fold, and the four Tier 3 failure modes that actually
  bite (letterboxing, lost scroll position, dropped text input, broken media playback on fold).
- **Target phrase:** "google play foldable screenshots requirement"
- **Links to:** `iphone-duo-app-ready-checklist`, `app-store-creative-assets-product-page-headers`,
  `google-play-contact-picker-location-button-deadline`

  being folded" story, one platform per post.
