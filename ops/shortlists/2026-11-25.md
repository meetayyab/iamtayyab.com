## 1. App Store age rating content descriptors — the Jan 12 deadline

- **Working title:** App Store Age Rating Content Descriptors: The January 12 Deadline Checklist
- **Why now:** App Store Connect added two content descriptors — **Shared Location** and
  **Dating and Relationships** — and *every* app must have them declared by
  **January 12, 2027**. Apple's App Store Connect release notes date the change
  **November 19, 2026**; Apple's developer news item (published November 2) says
  **November 16**. Sources disagree on the start date — flag that in the post.
  Primary sources opened: Apple developer news (Nov 2, 2026) and App Store Connect
  release notes (Nov 19, 2026).
- **Reader value:** A decide-and-declare checklist for anyone shipping an iOS app: which
  features trip each descriptor, that Dating and Relationships forces a **minimum 18+**
  rating, that Shared Location may not change the minimum in some regions, and the Brazil
  carve-out (declaring Shared Location auto-assigns **A16**, Dating and Relationships
  **A18**, with an MJSP official-rating route for genuine cases like parent–child location
  sharing). Covers the "my app has a share-my-location feature, am I now 18+?" panic.
- **Target phrase:** "App Store age rating content descriptors"
- **Links to:** `google-play-contact-picker-location-button-deadline`,
  `apple-eu-unified-app-store-terms-indie-guide`,
  `app-store-review-process-what-nobody-tells-you`
- **Sources:** https://developer.apple.com/news/ · https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/

## 2. App Store price and tax changes landing December 3

- **Working title:** App Store Price and Tax Changes: What Hits Your Proceeds on December 3
- **Why now:** Apple's **November 17, 2026** tax and price update: storefront prices are
  re-equalised in **Botswana, Colombia, Egypt, Rwanda and Türkiye effective December 3,
  2026**; VAT introduced in Botswana (14%) and Rwanda (18%), Significant Economic Presence
  tax in Cameroon and Côte d'Ivoire (3% each); a new **Software as a Service** tax
  attribute for App Store Software, Software Training Material, Dating and Boosting; and
  **California sales and use tax on SaaS from January 1, 2027**. Primary source opened.
- **Reader value:** What actually changes vs what only looks like it changes — auto-renewable
  subscriptions are excluded everywhere, manually-managed storefronts are untouched, your
  base storefront is untouched, and US displayed prices and proceeds don't move even though
  customers may pay more. Plus how to pick the right base storefront and review the new SaaS
  attribute in Pricing and Availability before December 3.
- **Target phrase:** "App Store price changes December 2026"
- **Links to:** `sell-the-year-bill-the-month-storekit`,
  `apple-eu-unified-app-store-terms-indie-guide`,

## 3. On-Demand Resources is closing — migrate to Background Assets

- **Working title:** Migrate On-Demand Resources to Background Assets Before Early 2027
- **Why now:** App Store Connect release note dated **November 12, 2026**: starting in
  **early 2027**, App Store Connect will stop accepting uploads containing On-Demand
  Resources from new apps, or from apps that have never used the feature. ODR is deprecated
  as of iOS/iPadOS/tvOS/visionOS 27, with support removed in future releases; Apple
  recommends **Background Assets**. Existing ODR apps keep working in the near term.
  Primary source opened.
- **Reader value:** A migration guide for apps and games that ship level packs, model files,
  offline maps or on-demand media: what Background Assets gives you, Apple-Hosted vs
  self-hosted, the download lifecycle, what to test, and the trap that this cutoff hits
  *new* apps first — so a rewrite shipping as a new bundle in 2027 cannot use ODR at all.
- **Target phrase:** "migrate On-Demand Resources to Background Assets"
- **Links to:** `iphone-duo-app-ready-checklist`,
  `app-store-review-process-what-nobody-tells-you`,
  `developer-id-certificate-expiry-2027-mac-apps`
- **Sources:** https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/ · https://developer.apple.com/app-store/whats-new/

## 4. macOS 27.3 splits Full Disk Access and Device Control (defence-only)

- **Working title:** macOS 27.3 Splits Full Disk Access: What Mac App Developers Should Fix
- **Why now:** Apple developer news, **November 16, 2026**: macOS 27.3 beta 2 splits Device
  Control and Data Access into **System & App Protection Override** and **Accessibility**,
  changes Full Disk Access so users grant access per app from a list and are steered toward
  granting only the specific folders a workflow needs, adds an ask-every-time option, and
  shows a transparency indicator when one app controls another. Previously granted
  permissions stay in effect; entitlements are available on request for backup apps and
  assistive technologies to keep the old experience on **new installations**.
  Primary source opened.
- **Reader value:** Defence-only: how to detect partial access instead of assuming
  all-or-nothing, degrade gracefully when only some folders are granted, write the
  onboarding copy that earns the grant, and decide whether you need to request an
  entitlement now rather than after your install numbers drop.
- **Target phrase:** "macOS Full Disk Access changes"
- **Links to:** `developer-id-certificate-expiry-2027-mac-apps`,
  `muse-ai-agent-hijack-privileged-assistant-checklist`,
  `mobile-remote-coding-agent-apps-security`
