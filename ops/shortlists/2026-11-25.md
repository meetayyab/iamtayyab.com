## 1. ⭐ The 2027 compliance bill — what a "finished" app actually costs to keep alive

- **Working title:** Your App Is "Finished". Here's the 2027 Bill Nobody Quoted You.
- **Why now:** five dated deadlines land between December 3, 2026 and April 2027, all
  verified on Apple's own pages this morning:
  - **Dec 3, 2026** — prices updated in Botswana, Colombia, Egypt, Rwanda, Türkiye.
  - **Jan 1, 2027** — California sales and use tax applies to SaaS, App Store Software,
    Software Training Material, Dating and Boosting.
  - **Jan 12, 2027** — *all* apps must have the new age rating declarations completed.
  - **early 2027** — App Store Connect stops accepting On-Demand Resources uploads from new
    apps and apps that never used the feature (ASC release notes, Nov 12, 2026).
  - **April 2027** — "any apps or games submitted will need to include screenshots for
    iPhone Duo."
  - ⚠️ Two more to re-verify before drafting: Developer ID Sub-CA expiry (Feb 1, 2027) and
    Google Play's location rules (Jan 27, 2027). Both appear in Tayyab's own published posts
    but could not be confirmed verbatim today — `support.google.com` is blocked and the
    Developer ID notice sits in an unread section of Apple's news page.
- **Reader value:** the founder who thinks the app is done learns the real recurring line
  item: which deadlines *block shipping* (age ratings, screenshots) versus which just *cost
  money* (tax, pricing), roughly what each costs in developer-days, and what belongs on a
  retainer versus ad hoc. Ends with an annual maintenance budget range.
- **Target phrase:** "app maintenance cost per year"
- **Links to:** `what-us-startups-pay-full-stack-pakistan`,
  `hire-pakistan-full-stack-contractor-retainer-dedicated` (the retainer case),
  `developer-id-certificate-expiry-2027-mac-apps`, hub `/full-stack-developer-pakistan`
- **Sources:** https://developer.apple.com/news/ · https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/ · https://developer.apple.com/app-store/whats-new/

## 2. Every app must re-declare its age rating by January 12 — one wrong answer makes it 18+

- **Working title:** One Checkbox Can Make Your App 18+: The January 12 Age Rating Deadline
- **Why now:** App Store Connect added **Shared Location** and **Dating and Relationships**
  descriptors, and **all apps must have them declared by January 12, 2027** (Apple's
  wording). Declaring Dating and Relationships forces a **minimum 18+** rating. In Brazil,
  Shared Location auto-assigns **A16** and Dating and Relationships **A18**, with an MJSP
  official-rating route for genuine cases like parent–child location sharing. Apple's
  developer news dates the rollout November 16; the App Store Connect release notes say
  November 19 — flag the disagreement in the post.
- **Reader value:** a founder whose app has a share-location, meetup or companion feature
  finds out whether it is about to be rated 18+, what that does to their addressable
  market, ad platforms and school/family channels, who on their team or contract should be
  answering the questionnaire, and that missing the date interrupts their ability to ship
  updates at all.
- **Target phrase:** "App Store age rating 18+"
- **Links to:** `app-store-review-process-what-nobody-tells-you`,
  `google-play-contact-picker-location-button-deadline`,
  `red-flags-hiring-full-stack-developer-pakistan` (nobody told you this was coming),
  hub `/full-stack-developer-pakistan`
- **Sources:** https://developer.apple.com/news/ · https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/

## 3. "We need to rebuild it" — telling a real forced migration from a padded quote

- **Working title:** "We Need to Rebuild It": How to Tell a Real Forced Migration From a Padded Quote
- **Why now:** the early-2027 Apple cutoffs are about to become the most legitimate-sounding
  rewrite argument in years. Some genuinely force code changes (On-Demand Resources), some
  are a screenshot or metadata job (iPhone Duo assets, age ratings), and one widely-repeated
  claim — that uploads must use the iOS 27 SDK from April 2027 — **could not be verified
  today**: Apple's own "upcoming requirements" page lists only the April 2026 Xcode 26 rule,
  so the 2027 version is a pattern, not a published fact. That gap is exactly the gap a
  padded quote lives in.
- **Reader value:** a four-question test to run on any rewrite proposal, a table sorting the
  2027 items into *code change* / *asset change* / *paperwork*, rough developer-day costs
  for each, and the exact wording to push back with when a deadline is being used to sell a
  rebuild. Evergreen: works for the next platform deadline too.
- **Target phrase:** "does my app need a rewrite"
- **Links to:** `saas-cancellation-wave-build-vs-buy`,
  `red-flags-hiring-full-stack-developer-pakistan`,
  `what-us-startups-pay-full-stack-pakistan`, hub `/full-stack-developer-pakistan`
- **Sources:** https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/ · https://developer.apple.com/app-store/whats-new/ · https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/

## 4. Your App Store revenue changes on December 3 and January 1

- **Working title:** Your App Store Revenue Changes December 3. Here's Exactly What Moves.
- **Why now:** Apple's **November 17, 2026** tax and price update. Prices update in five
  storefronts on **December 3**; VAT introduced in Botswana (14%) and Rwanda (18%);
  Significant Economic Presence tax in Cameroon and Côte d'Ivoire (3% each); a new
  **Software as a Service** tax attribute to review under Pricing and Availability; and
  **California sales and use tax from January 1, 2027**.
- **Reader value:** the short version is "less than you fear, but check three things" —
  auto-renewable subscriptions don't change in any region, your base storefront doesn't
  change, manually-managed storefronts aren't touched, and in the US your displayed price
  and your proceeds stay flat even though customers may pay more. The post says who does
  need to act before December 3 and what to look at in App Store Connect.
- **Target phrase:** "App Store price changes December 2026"
- **Links to:** `pricing-page-aeo-indie-saas-checklist`,
  `sell-the-year-bill-the-month-storekit`,
  `apple-eu-unified-app-store-terms-indie-guide`,

## 5. What to put in the contract so platform deadlines aren't your emergency

- **Working title:** The Maintenance Clauses Founders Forget — Until a Platform Deadline Hits
- **Why now:** evergreen buyer question, pegged to the Dec 2026–Apr 2027 deadline run that
  is about to generate a wave of "that wasn't in scope" conversations. *Why now* explains why
  the question keeps coming up rather than what shipped this week.
- **Reader value:** the clauses that actually matter when a platform moves the goalposts —
  who is responsible for *monitoring* platform changes, response time for a
  ship-blocking deadline, who pays for compliance work that wasn't in the original scope,
  and the one most founders get wrong: **who holds the Apple and Google accounts, the signing
  certificates and the DNS**. Plus what a clean handover looks like if the relationship ends.
- **Target phrase:** "app development contract maintenance clause"
- **Links to:** `hire-pakistan-full-stack-contractor-retainer-dedicated`,
