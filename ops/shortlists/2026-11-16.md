## 1. App Store age rating content descriptors land today — the Jan 12 declaration checklist

- **Why now:** Apple announced on November 2, 2026 that *"Starting November 16, 2026,
  App Store Connect will include new content descriptors"* — two of them, **Shared Location**
  and **Dating and Relationships**. That is today. All apps must have the declarations
  completed by **January 12, 2027**.
  Primary source: [Apple Developer News, Nov 2, 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yxkh08us)
- **Reader value:** A decision table for indie and small-team app developers: does your app
  trigger either descriptor (a "share my location with a friend" feature counts), what the
  rating consequence is (Dating and Relationships forces a **minimum 18+**; Shared Location
  may not change the minimum in some regions), the Brazil special case (A16 for Shared
  Location, A18 for Dating and Relationships, with an MJSP appeal route for parent-child
  location sharing), and when the questionnaire fires on your next update.
- **Target phrase:** "App Store age rating descriptors"

- **Why now:** Apple shipped retention messages for auto-renewable subscriptions on

## 3. Seven Next.js cache advisories: the self-hosted triage list

- **Why now:** Next.js published **seven Moderate advisories on November 3, 2026**, patched in
  **16.4.5** and **15.5.29**. Five are cache-boundary bugs — cross-user disclosure and poisoning
  in `use cache` with binary arguments, information disclosure across root params when a
  `use cache` function is called through `unstable_cache()`, fallback-shell poisoning in Cache
  Components — plus HttpOnly cookie exposure on Edge runtime routes and an SVG rejection bypass
  in image optimization. 16.4.6 followed on November 9 with route-handler compression and
  Turbopack GC fixes.
  Primary source: [Next.js security advisories](https://github.com/vercel/next.js/security/advisories)
- **Reader value:** Defence-only triage for small teams self-hosting Next.js: which advisory
  applies to which caching pattern, how to tell if you use the affected combination, the upgrade
  path, and what to check after upgrading. Cache bugs are the one class where a multi-tenant SaaS
  leaks other customers' data without anyone attacking it.
- **Target phrase:** "Next.js cache security advisories"
- **Links to:** `nextjs-16-3-turbopack-chunking`,
  `building-a-blog-engine-with-nextjs-16-app-router`,
  `ai-agent-hacking-small-saas-hardening-checklist`

## 4. Siri AI opened to every app — what to build now, ship in spring

- **Why now:** Apple's **November 3, 2026** Developer Preview opens Siri AI to all developers via
  App Intents in the iOS/iPadOS/macOS/visionOS 27.3 betas, adds **skills** for chaining several
  actions, and lets Siri AI surface *"any entity in the Spotlight semantic index, not just the
  ones that fit an existing schema."* App Store submission opens **in the spring**; Siri AI is
  currently unavailable in the EU on iOS, iPadOS and watchOS.
  Primary source: [Apple Developer News, Nov 3, 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=gdk2umrf)
- **Reader value:** A build-now/ship-later plan: which three or four actions in your app are worth
  exposing as intents, how skills differ from stacking intents, why TestFlight and App Store users
  see none of it yet, what the EU gap means for your roadmap, and the unresolved part Apple itself
  flagged — terms and usage policies are still to come.
- **Target phrase:** "Siri AI App Intents"

## 5. Effort levels, advisors and orchestrators: picking a coding-agent model in late 2026

- **Why now:** Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 shipped **November 9, 2026** with five named
  **effort levels** (Low → Max, Medium the default), an **advisor** pattern where a cheaper model
  consults a stronger one, and an **orchestrator** pattern delegating down. Pricing is
  **$8/$40 per million tokens**, fast mode **$16/$80** for up to 2.5× token generation; Fable 5.2
  uses fewer tokens than 5.1 at Medium.
  Primary source: [Anthropic, Nov 9, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)
- **Reader value:** The practical cost-and-routing question every solo builder now has: which
  effort level for which task, when a cheap model plus an advisor call beats one expensive model,
  what fast mode actually buys at double the price, and how to keep a month's agent spend
  predictable. Vendor-neutral framing — the patterns outlive this release.
- **Target phrase:** "coding agent model selection"
