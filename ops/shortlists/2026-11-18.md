## 1. App Store price and tax changes — the base-storefront playbook

- **Working title:** App Store Price and Tax Changes: What Hits Your Proceeds on December 3
- **Why now:** Apple published "Tax and price updates for apps, In-App Purchases, and
  subscriptions" on **November 17, 2026** — new VAT in Botswana (14%) and Rwanda (18%),
  new 3% Significant Economic Presence tax in Cameroon and Côte d'Ivoire, automatic price
  updates in Botswana, Colombia, Egypt, Rwanda and Türkiye **effective December 3, 2026**,

## 2. macOS 27.3 splits Full Disk Access and Device Control

- **Working title:** macOS Full Disk Access Changes in 27.3: What Mac App Onboarding Has to Handle Now
- **Why now:** Apple's **November 16, 2026** developer note, shipped with **macOS 27.3
  beta 2**, splits "Device Control and Data Access" into **System & App Protection
  Override** and **Accessibility**, scopes Full Disk Access to specific folders, adds an
  ask-every-time option and a visible indicator when one app is controlling another.
  Existing grants stay in effect; entitlements for backup and assistive-tech apps are
  "coming soon".
  Source: https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=oqng0zjk
- **Reader value:** A defence-only walkthrough: how to re-do permission onboarding, how to
  degrade gracefully with partial folder access, which apps should request an entitlement,
  and what the new transparency indicator means for anything that drives another app
  (including local AI assistants and agent tooling).
- **Target phrase:** "macOS Full Disk Access changes"
- **Links to:** `developer-id-certificate-expiry-2027-mac-apps`,
  `muse-ai-agent-hijack-privileged-assistant-checklist`, `mobile-remote-coding-agent-apps-security`

## 3. App Store retention messages are live — and App Review has rules

- **Working title:** App Store Retention Messages: The Cancellation-Flow Setup Apple Will Actually Approve
- **Why now:** Apple shipped retention messages for auto-renewable subscriptions on
  **November 4, 2026**, and the App Store Connect help doc is now complete enough to build
  against: **max 10 messages per app**, one message per subscription at a time, required
  header + body, optional image at **3840×2160 or 5244×2950**, up to 10 retention offers
  per billing plan type, Paid Subscription Duration and Wait Between Offers eligibility
  (wait is **per offer, not per subscription**), and an explicit App Review rule that the
  message text **must not mention promotions, discounts or prices**.
  Sources: https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=fk9ps3gv ·
  https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/configure-retention-messages/
- **Reader value:** A build-it checklist for indie subscription apps: what to write when you
  can't say "50% off", how to describe what a subscriber loses without being coercive,
  localisation per storefront, the draft/review flow (editing a live message creates a new
  version and you can't roll back), and when the Retention Messaging API is worth requesting.
- **Target phrase:** "App Store retention messages"
- **Links to:** `sell-the-year-bill-the-month-storekit`, `pricing-page-aeo-indie-saas-checklist`,
  `custom-billing-engine-stripe-subscriptions`
- **Primary source opened:** yes — news item plus the full App Store Connect help page.

## 5. Android 17's three-hour OTP delay

- **Working title:** Android 17 SMS OTP Delay: Fix Your Login Flow Before You Target API 37
- **Why now:** Android 17 (API level 37) withholds `SMS_RECEIVED_ACTION` and filters SMS
  provider queries for **three hours** on OTP messages. The WebOTP-format delay applies to
  **all apps regardless of target API level** when the app isn't the domain-verified
  recipient; the standard-SMS extension applies to apps targeting API 37+. Same wave as
  Google's October 29, 2026 policy update, which adds a Play Console declaration for
  `ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK` on API 37+.
  Source: https://developer.android.com/about/versions/17/behavior-changes-all ·
  https://developer.android.com/about/versions/17/release-notes
- **Reader value:** Why an auto-fill OTP login silently stalls on Android 17, how to move to
  SMS Retriever or SMS User Consent, which apps are exempt, and the rest of the API 37
  blast radius worth testing at the same time (app memory limits, per-app keystore cap of
  50,000 keys, cross-profile loopback block, background audio hardening).
- **Target phrase:** "Android 17 SMS OTP delay"
- **Links to:** `google-play-contact-picker-location-button-deadline`,
  `android-developer-verification-sept-30`, `google-play-12-tester-rule-indie`
- **Primary source opened:** yes — Android 17 behaviour-changes and release-notes pages read.
  Note: `support.google.com` (the Oct 29 Play policy announcement) is blocked, so Play
  Console declaration dates would be framed from `developer.android.com` only.
