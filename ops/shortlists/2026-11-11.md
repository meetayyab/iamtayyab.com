## 1. Apple's subscription retention messages are now open to everyone

- **Why now:** Apple made retention messages available to all developers in App Store
  Connect on **2026-11-04**; they render inside the subscription cancellation flow and
  can carry text, an image, or a special offer. The server-side Retention Messaging API
  is still access-by-request.
  Source: <https://developer.apple.com/news/> (2026-11-04 item) ·
  <https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging/>
- **Reader value:** A save-flow playbook for indie subscription apps: what to configure
  in App Store Connect (eligibility rules, wait time between offers), which offer to show
  which cohort, how to measure whether a save is incremental or just a delayed churn, and
  when a discount is the wrong answer. For solo founders running auto-renewables.
- **Target phrase:** "subscription retention messages"
- **Links to:** `sell-the-year-bill-the-month-storekit`,
  `pricing-page-aeo-indie-saas-checklist`, `custom-billing-engine-stripe-subscriptions`

## 2. Siri AI opened to all developers — the App Intents checklist

- **Why now:** Apple opened the Siri AI developer preview to all developers on
  **2026-11-03**, starting with the iOS/iPadOS/macOS/visionOS **27.3** betas. Apps hook in
  through **App Intents**, and a new "skills" feature bundles several actions into one
  experience. Ships to customers "in a few months"; **App Store submission opens in
  spring**. Not available in the EU on iOS/iPadOS/watchOS, not available under 13, and
  some server-side features carry daily usage limits.
  Source: <https://developer.apple.com/news/> (2026-11-03 item) ·
  <https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/siri-ai-developer-preview>
- **Reader value:** A new discovery surface that rewards early movers. What to expose as
  an App Intent, how to shape a "skill" rather than a pile of actions, what the EU and
  under-13 carve-outs mean if that is your market, and a realistic timeline for being
  ready when submissions open in spring.
- **Target phrase:** "Siri AI App Intents"
- **Links to:** `ai-app-discovery-ask-play-chatgpt-aso`, `iphone-duo-app-ready-checklist`,
  `make-your-saas-api-agent-ready`

## 3. App Store age ratings change Nov 16 — and every app must answer by Jan 12

- **Why now:** From **2026-11-16** App Store Connect adds two declarations to the age
  rating questionnaire: **Shared Location** (sharing real-world location with another
  person or group) and **Dating and Relationships**. Every app must have them completed by
  **2027-01-12**. Dating and Relationships forces a **minimum 18+** rating; in **Brazil**
  Shared Location requires **A16** and Dating and Relationships **A18**, with an appeal
  route via Brazil's Ministry of Justice for cases like parent–child location sharing.
  Source: <https://developer.apple.com/news/> (2026-11-02 item)
- **Reader value:** The deadline post format that already works on this site. Who is
  unexpectedly caught (any app with a "share my location with a friend" feature), what the
  18+ floor does to your addressable audience and ASO, the Brazil exception and how to
  request a lower official rating, and a dated checklist to clear before Jan 12.
- **Target phrase:** "App Store age rating descriptors"
- **Links to:** `google-play-contact-picker-location-button-deadline`,
  `app-store-review-process-what-nobody-tells-you`,
  `apple-eu-unified-app-store-terms-indie-guide`

## 4. Cutting the bill on a long agent loop: message threads and the 5.2 price drop

- **Why now:** On **2026-11-09** Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.2 at **$8/MTok input and
  $40/MTok output**, down from **$10/$50** for 5.1 — a 20% cut — with a fast mode at
  $16/$80 for up to 2.5x output tokens/sec. It pairs with **message threads** (beta,
  `message-threads-2026-08-12`, **2026-11-02**), which keeps the conversation server-side so
  each later request sends only new messages: smaller requests and lower time to first
  token. Threads expire 5 minutes after the last request and are deleted within an hour.
  Source: <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/release-notes/api> (2026-11-09, 2026-11-02 entries)
- **Reader value:** Where the money actually goes in an agent loop, worked with real
  per-token numbers: what message threads do and do not save, why the 5-minute expiry
  makes them wrong for a human-in-the-loop queue, when fast mode's 2x price is worth it,
  and how to instrument cost per run before you optimise.
- **Target phrase:** "reduce agent API token costs"
- **Links to:** `make-your-saas-api-agent-ready`,
  `ai-first-development-build-features-faster`, `mobile-remote-coding-agent-apps-security`
- **Note:** adjacent to `make-your-saas-api-agent-ready` but that post is about exposing
  your own API to agents; this is about the cost of running one.

## 5. IP-allowlisting your model API keys: shrinking the blast radius of a leak

- **Why now:** Anthropic added **IP allowlisting** in beta on **2026-11-05** — up to
  **1,000 IPv4/IPv6 addresses or CIDR ranges per organization**, so requests from
  off-list addresses are refused and a leaked key is far less useful. The same entry adds
  a Compliance API security-posture endpoint and an admin "sign out everywhere" action.
  Source: <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/release-notes/api> (2026-11-05 entry) ·
  <https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/ip-allowlist>
- **Reader value:** Defence-only key hygiene for a small backend: what an allowlist does
  and does not cover, how to get a stable egress IP when you are on serverless (the hard
  part — Vercel/Lambda egress is not fixed by default), what to do instead if you cannot,
  and a rotation drill to run before you need it.
- **Target phrase:** "API key IP allowlist"
- **Links to:** `ai-agent-hacking-small-saas-hardening-checklist`,
  `plugin4shell-ai-coding-agent-security`, `mobile-remote-coding-agent-apps-security`
- **Note:** narrower than the existing hardening checklist, and would link to it as hub.
