## 1. App Store age rating content descriptors (Shared Location, Dating and Relationships)

- **Why now:** Apple's App Store Connect release notes say the age rating questionnaire
  gained two new content descriptors — **Shared Location** and **Dating and Relationships** —
  on **19 Nov 2026**, and Apple's 2 Nov developer news item gave **16 Nov 2026** as the start
  date (the two Apple pages disagree; the post should say so). Every app must have the
  declarations completed by **12 Jan 2027**, and they are required on any update submission or
  questionnaire change in the meantime.
  Sources: [App Store Connect release notes](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/),
  [Apple Developer news, 2 Nov 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/).
- **Reader value:** A walk-through of the two new declarations for solo iOS developers:
  what counts as "Shared Location" (any user-to-user location sharing, not Maps-style
  self-location), why Dating and Relationships forces a minimum **18+** rating, the Brazil
  rules (**A16** for Shared Location, **A18** for Dating and Relationships) and how a
  parent-child location-sharing app can petition Brazil's MJSP for a lower official rating.
  Ends with what to answer, what it does to your rating, and the 12 Jan deadline.
- **Target phrase:** "app store age rating content descriptors"
- **Links to:** `app-store-review-process-what-nobody-tells-you`,
  `apple-eu-unified-app-store-terms-indie-guide`, `iphone-duo-app-ready-checklist`

## 2. Google Play's adaptive quality bar and the foldable screenshot requirement

- **Why now:** Google Play announced new **adaptive app quality requirements** on
  **18 Nov 2026**. From **April 2027** apps must meet at least **Tier 3 (Adaptive ready)**
  or they can no longer publish or update on Play and may lose store visibility, and new
  submissions/listing updates must carry **3 foldable screenshots** on top of the standard
  2 phone screenshots. Foldable screenshot upload is **live in Play Console today**.
  **August 2027** adds a target **API level 37 (Android 17)** requirement.
  Source: [Elevating apps: New adaptive quality standards on Google Play](https://developer.android.com/blog/posts/google-play-adaptive-quality-standards).
- **Reader value:** The concrete checklist most indie Android apps will fail: no
  letterboxing or compatibility mode, state preserved across rotate/fold/unfold/resize,
  multi-window support, plus the exact screenshot aspect ratios (1:1 and 4:3 inner
  displays, 10:16 folded outer, optional 16:9 landscape) and how to capture them free from
  the Android Emulator rather than buying a Fold. Tiers 2 and 1 explained as the badging
  upside.
- **Target phrase:** "google play foldable screenshot requirements"
- **Links to:** `google-play-12-tester-rule-indie`,
  `google-play-contact-picker-location-button-deadline`, `android-developer-verification-sept-30`

## 3. Apple's 17 Nov tax and price update, and the SaaS tax attribute

- **Why now:** Apple's **17 Nov 2026** developer news post adds VAT in Botswana (14%) and
  Rwanda (18%) and Significant Economic Presence tax in Cameroon and Côte d'Ivoire (3%),
  introduces a new **"Software as a Service (SaaS)" tax attribute** in Pricing and
  Availability, and warns that from **1 Jan 2027** App Store Software, Software Training
  Material, Dating and Boosting become subject to **California sales and use tax**. Storefront
  price updates land **3 Dec 2026** for Botswana, Colombia, Egypt, Rwanda and Türkiye.
  Source: [Apple Developer news, 17 Nov 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/).
- **Reader value:** What a one-person app business actually has to do before 3 Dec and
  1 Jan: pick the right tax attribute for a subscription app, understand that US prices are
  displayed tax-exclusive so the customer's total rises while your proceeds do not, decide
  whether to re-pin manually managed prices, and know that auto-renewable subscriptions are
  excluded from the automatic price update.

## 4. macOS 27.3 splits Device Control and Data Access — what automation and assistant apps must change

- **Why now:** Apple's **16 Nov 2026** developer news post, shipped with **macOS 27.3
  beta 2**, splits Device Control and Data Access into **System & App Protection Override**
  and **Accessibility** settings. Users now pick the requesting app from a list, can force an
  app to ask every time, and see a transparency indicator while one app controls another.
  Apple tells developers to prepare for access to only a limited set of folders or apps, and
  says backup apps using Full Disk Access and assistive apps using Accessibility can request
  entitlements for new installations; existing installs keep today's behaviour for now.
  Source: [Apple Developer news, 16 Nov 2026](https://developer.apple.com/news/).
- **Reader value:** Defence-only. For anyone shipping a Mac utility, backup tool, clipboard
  manager or local AI assistant: how to degrade gracefully when you hold partial folder
  access, how to stop assuming Full Disk Access is permanent, which entitlement path applies
  to which app type, and why "one app driving another" is now visible to the user — which is
  also the honest security upside after this year's agent-hijack incidents.
- **Target phrase:** "macos full disk access changes for developers"
- **Links to:** `developer-id-certificate-expiry-2027-mac-apps`,
  `muse-ai-agent-hijack-privileged-assistant-checklist`, `mobile-remote-coding-agent-apps-security`

## 5. Android 17 (API 37) is now on the clock — the auth and security changes that break first

- **Why now:** Google Play's **18 Nov 2026** adaptive-quality post sets **August 2027** as the
  date new and updated apps must target **API level 37**. The Android 17 behaviour-change docs
  show what flips on that day, and the sharpest one is **SMS OTP protection**: for most apps
  targeting API 37, a standard SMS containing a one-time code is withheld for **three hours** —
  `SMS_RECEIVED_ACTION` is not broadcast and SMS provider queries are filtered. Also landing:
  `ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK` becomes a mandatory runtime permission, certificate transparency is on
  by default, native libraries loaded with `System.load()` must be read-only, and `static final`
  fields can no longer be changed by reflection.
  Sources: [Behavior changes for apps targeting Android 17](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/17/behavior-changes-17),
  [Google Play adaptive quality standards](https://developer.android.com/blog/posts/google-play-adaptive-quality-standards).
- **Reader value:** A migration checklist for small teams who own both an app and its
  backend: move off SMS-scraping to SMS Retriever or SMS User Consent before the three-hour
  delay silently breaks sign-in, adopt system device pickers instead of asking for local
  network access, and audit any reflection or dynamic native loading in your dependencies.
  Defence-only throughout.
- **Target phrase:** "targeting android 17 api 37 changes"
- **Links to:** `ai-agent-hacking-small-saas-hardening-checklist`, `google-play-12-tester-rule-indie`,
  `google-play-contact-picker-location-button-deadline`
