## 1. Apple's new age rating questionnaire: Shared Location and Dating and Relationships

- **Working title:** App Store Age Rating Questionnaire: The Two New Declarations Every App Must Answer by January 12
- **Why now:** The two new content descriptors went live in App Store Connect on **November 19, 2026**
  ([App Store Connect release notes](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/)),
  after Apple's **November 2, 2026** heads-up that the questionnaire changes start November 16 and that
  *"All apps must have these declarations completed by January 12, 2027"*
  ([Apple Developer news](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=yxkh08us)). Dating and Relationships forces a
  minimum **18+** rating; in Brazil, Shared Location forces **A16** and Dating and Relationships **A18**.
- **Reader value:** What the two descriptors actually cover (including "a virtual companion that engages a
  user in sexual or romantic content" — which catches a lot of AI companion apps), the ordinary features
  that trip Shared Location (friend finders, group trip planning, live ETA sharing), what an 18+ rating
  costs you in reach and in parental controls, the Brazil MJSP route for a lower official rating, and a
  20-minute pass through the questionnaire before the January 12 cutoff. For anyone shipping an iOS app.
- **Target phrase:** "App Store age rating questionnaire"
- **Links to:** `app-store-review-process-what-nobody-tells-you`,
  `google-play-contact-picker-location-button-deadline`, `app-store-creative-assets-product-page-headers`

## 2. macOS 27.3 splits Full Disk Access — and your Mac app may only get three folders

- **Working title:** macOS 27.3 Changes Full Disk Access: What Mac App and Agent Developers Should Fix Now
- **Why now:** Apple announced on **November 16, 2026**, alongside macOS 27.3 beta 2, that Device Control and
  Data Access splits into **System & App Protection Override** and **Accessibility**, that users will be
  steered to grant Full Disk Access to *"just the specific apps … or folders"*, that they can set an app to
  **ask every time**, and that macOS will show a transparency indicator when one app drives another
  ([Apple Developer news](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=oqng0zjk)).
- **Reader value:** A defence-side read: how to onboard when you may only be handed a few folders, what
  breaks for backup, automation and screen-reading apps, the entitlement route Apple is opening for backup
  and assistive apps, and — for the many builders now running coding agents on their own Macs — how to use
  per-folder grants and ask-every-time to shrink what a hijacked agent can reach.
- **Target phrase:** "macOS Full Disk Access changes"
- **Links to:** `muse-ai-agent-hijack-privileged-assistant-checklist`,
  `mobile-remote-coding-agent-apps-security`, `developer-id-certificate-expiry-2027-mac-apps`,
  `plugin4shell-ai-coding-agent-security`

## 3. December 3 price reset, January 1 California tax: the App Store pricing week

- **Working title:** App Store Price Updates on December 3: What Changes, What Doesn't, and the Tax Attribute to Check
- **Why now:** Apple's **November 17, 2026** notice: VAT introduced in Botswana (14%) and Rwanda (18%), SEP
  tax in Cameroon and Côte d'Ivoire (3%), **prices change in Botswana, Colombia, Egypt, Rwanda and Türkiye on
  December 3, 2026**, California sales and use tax applies to SaaS, App Store Software, Software Training

- **Reader value:** The base-storefront mechanics (why your prices move everywhere *except* your base), why
  auto-renewable subscriptions are excluded and what that does to a mixed one-time/subscription catalogue,
  how to pick the right tax category and what the wrong one costs, and the ten-minute check to run in App

## 4. Next.js is rewriting `next start` in Go, and it now has a name

- **Working title:** Next.js Is Moving `next start` to Go: What Switchyard Means for Self-Hosting
- **Why now:** The experimental `next start` front server was renamed **Switchyard** on **November 19, 2026**
  ([PR #102035](https://github.com/vercel/next.js/pull/102035)), got the app's route and worker paths on
  Nov 21 ([#102101](https://github.com/vercel/next.js/pull/102101)), stopped guessing Content-Type after 103
  Early Hints and had `proxy.go` restricted to App Router apps on Nov 23
  ([#102011](https://github.com/vercel/next.js/pull/102011)), and switched to resetting rather than closing
  connections to Node on Nov 24 ([#102164](https://github.com/vercel/next.js/pull/102164)). It is shipping
  behind flags through the 16.5 canaries ([releases](https://github.com/vercel/next.js/releases)).
- **Reader value:** What a Go front process in front of Node actually changes for a self-hosted Next.js app,
  which assumptions break first (proxy/middleware behaviour, streaming, header and compression handling,
  Early Hints), how to try it on a staging deploy without betting your production box on a canary, and what
  to watch before 16.5 goes stable. The honest version: this is a canary, not a release.
- **Target phrase:** "Next.js Go server next start"
- **Links to:** `nextjs-16-3-turbopack-chunking`, `building-a-blog-engine-with-nextjs-16-app-router`,
  `frontend-after-ai-what-still-matters`, `fixing-lighthouse-score-portfolio-seo-accessibility`

## 5. App Store Connect's new App Compliance page is where AI-content declarations now live

- **Working title:** App Store Connect's New App Compliance Page: Where AI Content Declarations Go Now
- **Why now:** On **November 16, 2026**, App Store Connect moved regulations and permits (ICP, game licences,
  medical device declarations) out of App Information into a new **App Compliance** page, which is now where
  you manage declarations for apps with **AI-generated or synthetic content (AIGC)** and features needing a
  Security Assessment in mainland China
  ([App Store Connect release notes](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/)).
- **Reader value:** Where each field moved, which indie apps actually owe an AIGC declaration (anything with
  generated images, voice, video or text output — more apps than their owners think), what to write and what
  not to over-claim, how this lines up with the AI-imagery tightening in guideline 1.2, and how a missing
  declaration shows up as a review rejection rather than a warning.
- **Target phrase:** "App Store AI generated content declaration"
- **Links to:** `building-in-public-ai-app-clones`, `app-store-review-process-what-nobody-tells-you`,
  `make-your-saas-api-agent-ready`, `ai-first-development-build-features-faster`
